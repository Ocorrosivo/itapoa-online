# Monopoly lens
Score: 3/10 | Vote: PASS

## What is strong
- **Small market is correctly scoped to dominate:** Itapoá (~80k, hiperlocal bairro/categoria) is exactly the kind of small market the Monopoly lens wants you to own first before expanding. If the business can get >50% of paying commerces in 2 bairros or 2 categories, the "small monopoly before scale" logic applies.
- **Bundling is a distribution edge over fragmented free tools:** Google Perfil + Instagram + WhatsApp Business + OLX together solve "be found," but separately. A single local funnel (destaque/busca → mini-site → WhatsApp lead capture) is not 10x tech, but it is a convenience bundle a busy comerciante may prefer over managing 4 tools.
- **Production risk is low, so time can go to distribution:** 37 tables, Traefik/Let's Encrypt, Mercado Pago webhook single `paid` path, 16 demo listings — engineering question is not "can it be built" but "can it be sold." That lets the team test distribution without more build.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **No durable advantage — commodity directory with four free substitutes that already win.** No proprietary technology 10x better on something that matters, no network effects yet, no economies of scale at 16 listings, and no local brand. Any imitator (another dev, a mídia local, Google itself) can copy the directory in weeks. In a crowded substitute market, competition destroys margin and price trends to free.
2. **Distribution is unproven and likely costs more than the price supports.** Reaching the first 100 payers door-to-door/WhatsApp in a seasonal city at R$ 49,90–99,90/month is the real product. With no measured CAC, cold start chicken-egg (merchants pay for visibility, but visibility needs traffic; traffic needs merchants), and seasonality, CAC can easily exceed 1–2 months of revenue. The Monopoly lens treats distribution as half the business — without a repeatable, paid-positive channel, there is no business.
3. **Durability fails on year 3–10:** Even if 20–30 merchants pay in 2026, what stops churn when none get leads in low season, or Google adds a "Itapoá" pack that outranks it, or a copycat undercuts to R$ 29? Talent question also unanswered: who does local consultative sales month after month in Itapoá? Without a moat built now, any early share is rented, not owned.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- What is the exact small market you intend to dominate first (2 categories or 2 bairros where you can claim >50% of payers), and why that wedge over the other 8 categories/20 bairros?
- Engineering: what is actually 10x better here vs. free Google Perfil + Instagram + WhatsApp? If nothing is 10x, which network effect or data flywheel will become the moat after 50 payers?
- Distribution: how do the first 100 payers arrive, step by step, and what does each one cost (visits, calls, time)? What CAC have you measured in a 10-pilot?
- Durability: what in 10 years still holds if Google changes ranking or a copycat launches at half price? Scale: how does unit economics improve from 30 to 300 payers (or does it not)?
- Timing: why now for Itapoá specifically — what changed in 2025–26 that makes a paid local directory winnable (turismo, porto, veraneio, busca local)?
- Secret: what do you know about Itapoá merchants buying behavior that an outsider copying this would not guess?
- People: who does the weekly grind of selling and servicing merchants locally, and do they have that skill now?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
This vote is PASS as pitched. It would move to FUND IF only if:
- Lock exclusive wedge: schriftlich own >50% of paying businesses in 1–2 categories or bairros within 90 days, with proof (paying list + traffic share), before expanding scope.
- Create one non-commodity moat: either a liquidity side (exclusive leads/reviews/vagas that Google does not give) or a switching-cost side (CRM + mini-site + real lead history merchants would lose by leaving).
- Prove distribution at CAC < 1/3 LTV on pilot data (`cfo` + `consumer` + `competitors` must confirm price/LTV and channel cost). No scale spend until that holds for 2 consecutive months.
- If these are not met, do not pursue — build a different small monopoly or do not build.
